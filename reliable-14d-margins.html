OSRS Reliable 14-Day Margins

A live 1-day margin snapshot can be a fluke — one seller's misprice, a brief supply gap. PocketGE's Reliable 14-Day Margins scanner instead looks for items whose Grand Exchange spread has stayed profitable, day after day, across the last two weeks: the flips worth setting up as a routine rather than chasing once.

How it's scored

For each candidate item, PocketGE pulls the last 14 days of Grand Exchange price history and computes the after-tax margin for every day in that window, then averages it. Items only qualify when the margin stays positive across enough of those days — a single good day surrounded by losses doesn't make the list. The result ranks by average daily gp profit, so what's shown is a realistic day-to-day expectation, not a best-case outlier.

This is the same 2% GE tax math used everywhere else on PocketGE — sell price − buy price − tax — applied consistently across 14 days rather than a single live print, so a name on this list has actually earned its spot.

Why this beats chasing a single snapshot

High Vol and Low Vol Margins (PocketGE's live lists) show you what's profitable right now — useful, but a spread that looks great this minute can vanish by the time you've placed an offer. Reliable 14-Day Margins trades a bit of upside for consistency: these are the items you can flip on a schedule without re-checking whether the opportunity is still real.
